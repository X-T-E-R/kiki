Settings › Models & providers › Connections now lets you add a provider from the models.dev directory. You can search it, and entries this version cannot reach show the reason. Providers without a fixed address ask for a base URL. Importing a provider that is already configured refreshes it. Signed-in accounts now show their remaining quota at the provider, with per-window limits for Kimi Code.
